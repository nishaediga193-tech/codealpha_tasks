System Architecture & Java OOP Design

Comprehensive domain model mapping rooms, guests, payments, and reservation lifecycles.

Hotel.java

Inventory manager for rooms (101–303). Supports filtered queries, booking reservations, and cancellation rollbacks.

Room.java

Contains room number, Category enum (Standard, Deluxe, Suite), price per night, and availability state.

Reservation.java

Binds a guest, room, stay duration, total calculated cost, and assigned confirmation UUID.

PaymentProcessor.java

Simulates payment authorization across Credit Card, Debit Card, UPI, and Net Banking channels.
